# Changelog

## 0.6.2 (6 October 2026)

- 0.6.2: era paintings upscaled to 3200×1800 for sharp display on retina and iPad screens.
- New Era I world-tree painting.
- New world paintings for Eras II–VII.

## 0.6.1 (6 October 2026)

- **World-tree look throughout.** The old line tree from 0.5 is retired. Each Era's tree page now shows its painting with clickable branches, and the unit picker, skill list and Era tabs share the painted look. Question cards keep their plain style, green Check button and colour themes.
- **Curtain intro.** Opening an Era from the front page pulls back a velvet curtain under gaslights. It plays only with Celebrations on Big, never when the device asks for reduced motion, and a click or any key skips it.
- **Era VI is "The Spiral Isle",** with a matching icon.

## 0.6 (6 October 2026)

New since the 0.5 beta:

- **A new front page.** Every visit opens on the seven Era worlds, which turn from grey to colour as you point at them. Click one to open its tree. A search box in the middle finds any skill or unit (press / or Ctrl/Cmd+K from anywhere).
- **Acorn save codes.** Your whole tree as one code or QR, with no account. Includes the Grafting Bench to load a tree, and Tree Rings to go back to one of your last 10 snapshots.
- **Treeprint and Field Guide.** Printable PDFs of a tree: a 3-page Treeprint (passport, a page for grown-ups, the Acorn) or a longer Field Guide of up to 10 pages. Colour or ink-saver styles. Any pasted Acorn can be printed too.
- **This week.** A card on the ID card showing the last week's activity and study habits.
- **Era worlds.** Each Era has a painting with its branches drawn on. Tap a branch to open that unit's skills.
- **Precalculus proof skills.** Ten new trig and conic skills that prove their formulas before using them, and proof steps on 11 existing levels.
- **Practice layout.** Questions take the full screen, plus "quick five" sets of small questions checked together.
- **Placement confirm checks.** A right answer on a skill you were placed past makes it growing, never proven straight away.
- **Celebrations and sounds,** both with an off switch.
- **Colour themes.** Ten themes, "Surprise me", and special-day themes such as Pi Day and Ada Lovelace Day.
- **DJ mode.** A classroom projector board of questions for groups to answer on paper.

Coming later: teacher and parent mode (the Satchel, assignments, linking codes), more precalculus (polar and parametric, matrices, plane vectors), and new skills.
